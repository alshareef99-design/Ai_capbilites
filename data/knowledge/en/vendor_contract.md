# Master Services Agreement — Cloud Hosting (excerpt)

**Parties:** the Company and Northwind Cloud LLC.

## Service levels

The vendor shall maintain monthly availability of **99.5%**. Service credits of **10%** of monthly fees apply if availability falls below 99.5% but not below 99.0%. Credits of **25%** apply below 99.0%.

## Data and security

The vendor must hold **ISO 27001** and **SOC 2 Type II**. Customer data remains Confidential. The vendor shall not transfer personal data outside the Kingdom without prior written consent.

## Liability

Liability is capped at **twelve months of fees** paid under this agreement, except for willful misconduct, data breach caused by the vendor, or infringement. There is no cap for those exceptions.

## Term and termination

The initial term is **36 months** starting 2026-01-01. Either party may terminate for material breach if not cured within **30 days** of written notice. The Company may terminate for convenience with **90 days** notice after month 18, subject to an early-termination fee of **20%** of remaining fees.

## Lock-in risk

Workloads use proprietary orchestration APIs. A full exit is estimated at **4 months** and **1.1 million SAR**.
