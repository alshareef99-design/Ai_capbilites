# Group Procurement Policy

This policy governs how the company buys goods and services.

## Approval thresholds

- Purchases up to **50,000 SAR** may be approved by a department manager.
- Purchases from **50,001 SAR to 250,000 SAR** require Finance Director approval and **three competitive quotes**.
- Purchases above **250,000 SAR** require the Procurement Committee and a written business case.

Single-source awards above 50,000 SAR are allowed only with a documented justification (sole supplier, emergency, or compatibility).

## Conflict of interest

Employees must disclose any personal relationship with a vendor. Failure to disclose is a compliance breach and may lead to disciplinary action.

## Vendor onboarding

New vendors must complete KYC, tax registration, and information-security review before the first purchase order is issued.

## Records

Quotes, approvals, and contracts are retained for **seven years**.
