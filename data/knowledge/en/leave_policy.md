# Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive:

- **21 calendar days** of annual leave after the first year of service.
- **30 calendar days** of sick leave per year with a medical certificate after three consecutive days.
- **10 calendar days** of Hajj leave once during employment for Muslim employees who have not performed Hajj before.
- Maternity leave is **10 weeks** paid; paternity leave is **3 days**.

Unused annual leave may be carried over up to **5 days** into the next year. Cash in lieu requires HR Director approval.

Leave requests are submitted in the HR system at least **five working days** in advance except for emergencies.
