# Information Security Policy

Information is classified as **Public**, **Internal**, **Confidential**, or **Restricted**.

## Access

- Production access requires **MFA**.
- Privileged access is reviewed every **90 days**.
- Sharing Restricted data outside the company requires Legal and CISO approval.

## Incidents

Security incidents must be reported to the SOC within **one hour**. Confirmed breaches of personal data are escalated to Legal within **24 hours**.

## Vendors

Vendors that process Confidential or Restricted data must hold **ISO 27001** or **SOC 2 Type II** and sign a data-processing addendum.

## Penalties

Willful bypass of MFA or unauthorized export of Restricted data is a high-severity compliance breach.
